LeadsMaps - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

  • Controller: David Amiguinho Unipessoal, tax ID (NIPC) 231867832, Caldas da Rainha, Portugal.
  • Privacy contact: david.amiguinho@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • LeadsMaps runs in your browser. The contacts it extracts stay on your computer and are never sent to us.
  • We only process your own data if you subscribe to the Pro plan: your email and the subscription status (section 7).
  • There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising or sale of data.

3. Roles in data processing

  • When you use the extension to collect business contacts, you are the controller of that data (GDPR, Art. 4(7)). You are responsible for complying with the GDPR, Law No. 58/2019 and Law No. 41/2004 (unsolicited electronic communications). We have no access to that data.
  • For the Pro subscription data (section 7), we are the controller.

4. Data processed locally in your browser

  • Fields the extension may collect, at your request, from the pages you open: company name, category, rating and number of reviews, address, postal code, city, phone numbers, emails, website, Google Maps URL, opening status, social media profiles (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, WhatsApp), NIF/NIPC (Portuguese tax ID), source URL and collection date.
  • Source: Google Maps results and websites you visit and (Pro, with your authorisation) the public contact pages of those websites.
  • Where it is stored: in the extension's storage in your Chrome profile (chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.session). Settings, the Free plan's daily counter, a cache of the subscription status and a cache of the robots.txt of the sites consulted are stored there too.

5. Network requests

The extension only makes these requests:

  • Company websites that you asked to enrich or analyse, and the robots.txt of those sites, always at your request, without cookies, without a referrer and with the browser's normal User-Agent. Access to each website is requested per site, through Chrome's permissions dialog, and is removed at the end of each operation.
  • ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), to find out whether you have Pro active and to open the payment and subscription management pages (section 7). These requests carry only a random installation identifier and the extension ID. No collected contact is sent.

The extension also runs a small script on extensionpay.com pages, which is only used to receive the payment confirmation. It does not read or send any other data from those pages.

6. Purpose and limited use (Chrome Web Store)

  • Data is used only to provide the features you ask for: extracting, organising and exporting contacts, and managing your subscription.
  • We do not sell data, we do not share it with third parties (other than the payment providers in section 7), and we do not use it for advertising, credit assessment or any other purpose unrelated to the extension.
  • Our use of information obtained through Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the "Limited Use" requirements.

7. Pro plan: payments and subscription

  • Who processes it: payment is made through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe to process cards. Card details are entered directly on the ExtensionPay/Stripe pages; neither the extension nor we ever see them.
  • What data: your email (requested at checkout and used to recover Pro in another browser through a link sent by email), the plan and the subscription status. Stripe processes payment and billing data (card, name, country, billing address) under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). According to ExtensionPay, the only identifying data it stores is the email address.
  • Identifier in the browser: the extension stores a random ExtensionPay identifier in chrome.storage.sync. If Chrome sync is on, this identifier is synchronised by Google across your browsers.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(b)) and compliance with legal invoicing obligations (point (c)).
  • Retention: while the subscription is active and, afterwards, for the period required by tax law for invoicing documents (10 years in Portugal).
  • International transfers: ExtensionPay and Stripe may process data outside the European Economic Area, notably in the USA. These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (GDPR, Articles 45 and 46).

8. Retention and deletion of local data

  • Local data is kept until you delete it: Options > Data > "Clear all data", or by deleting individual lists.
  • On the Free plan, the last extraction only lasts until you close the browser.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of the extension's local storage.
  • Files you export (CSV, Excel, JSON) are under your control and are not deleted by the extension.

9. Your rights

  • Regarding subscription data: you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection through david.amiguinho@gmail.com. You can cancel the subscription at any time in Options > License > "Manage subscription".
  • Regarding the contacts you collected with the extension: as controller, you are responsible for responding to data subjects (information at first contact, Art. 14; objection, Art. 21; erasure, Art. 17) and for keeping a do-not-contact list.
  • You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD, www.cnpd.pt).

10. Security

  • No remote code; a restrictive content security policy; minimal permissions (activeTab, scripting, storage, downloads; access to Google Maps and extensionpay.com; access to other websites only when you authorise it, site by site).
  • Local data is not encrypted by the extension: it is protected by the security of your device and your Chrome profile. Exports are stored in plain form on your disk.

11. Other

  • The extension is not intended for children under 16 and must not be used to collect special-category data (GDPR Art. 9) or data about minors.
  • Changes to this policy will be published at this URL with a new date. Significant changes will be flagged in the extension.
